# User Personas

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary users Mezgeb is designed for and describes their goals, behaviors, frustrations, and needs.

Personas help guide product and UX decisions by keeping the design centered around the actual learning experience rather than treating Mezgeb as a generic productivity application.

---

# 2. Primary Persona — The Independent Technical Learner

### Profile

**Name:** Hana
**Age:** 20–25
**Background:** University student or early-career developer
**Learning style:** Self-directed and project-based

Hana is actively learning technical subjects outside of formal coursework. She may be studying topics such as React, backend development, databases, system design, DSA, cloud technologies, or AI.

She usually follows multiple learning resources at the same time and works toward long-term learning goals.

### Goals

* Track what she is currently learning.
* Break large learning goals into manageable milestones.
* Know what she has already completed.
* Record study sessions and learning time.
* Reflect on what she learned.
* See progress toward long-term goals.
* Maintain consistency without turning learning into a productivity competition.

### Behaviors

* Studies several topics simultaneously.
* Uses roadmaps, tutorials, documentation, courses, and projects.
* Takes notes while learning.
* Creates personal task lists.
* Often switches between resources.
* Works on learning goals over weeks or months.
* May forget what was learned during previous sessions.
* Wants evidence of progress when a goal feels overwhelming.

### Frustrations

* Learning information is scattered across different tools.
* Generic task managers do not capture the learning process.
* Completing a task does not necessarily mean understanding a topic.
* It can be difficult to remember what was studied previously.
* Long-term goals can feel overwhelming without visible progress.
* Existing productivity tools often emphasize getting things done rather than understanding and reflection.

### Needs from Mezgeb

* A clear place to organize learning goals.
* Milestones for breaking large goals into stages.
* Tasks for concrete learning actions.
* Learning sessions for recording actual study time.
* Journal entries for reflection.
* Progress views that show meaningful learning activity.
* A simple dashboard that makes the current learning state obvious.

### Example

Hana creates:

**Goal:** Learn React

**Milestones:**

1. React Fundamentals
2. Hooks
3. Routing
4. State Management
5. Forms
6. Testing
7. Final Project

She studies React for two hours, records the session, completes several tasks, and writes a short reflection about what she struggled with.

Over several weeks, Mezgeb gives her a record of how the goal developed rather than only showing a list of completed tasks.

# 3. Secondary Persona — The Structured Student

### Profile

**Name:** Dawit
**Age:** 18–24
**Background:** University student
**Learning style:** Curriculum-oriented

Dawit learns primarily through university courses but also studies independently to strengthen his skills.

He wants a structured way to keep track of academic and personal learning goals.

### Goals

* Organize multiple learning goals.
* Keep track of study sessions.
* Maintain consistency during the semester.
* Record what was learned.
* Review progress over time.
* Balance university learning with personal technical development.

### Behaviors

* Has several simultaneous subjects or learning goals.
* Studies according to deadlines and schedules.
* Uses notebooks, documents, or task applications.
* Occasionally reviews previous material.
* May create short-term goals around exams or assignments.

### Frustrations

* University coursework and personal learning are often tracked separately.
* Important learning activity can become difficult to remember.
* Traditional task lists do not provide much context about learning.
* Progress can be difficult to visualize across several subjects.

### Needs from Mezgeb

* Multiple independent goals.
* Simple task and milestone organization.
* Session tracking.
* Progress summaries.
* Journal/reflection functionality.
* A dashboard that provides a quick overview of active learning.

# 4. Secondary Persona — The Long-Term Skill Builder

### Profile

**Name:** Meron
**Age:** 22–30
**Background:** Self-taught developer or early-career professional
**Learning style:** Long-term and project-oriented

Meron is continuously developing technical skills alongside work or other responsibilities.

Instead of following one short course, she may spend several months developing a broader skill set.

### Goals

* Track long-term learning journeys.
* Maintain consistency over months.
* Measure progress across different areas.
* Document lessons learned.
* Review previous learning experiences.
* Build a personal history of development.

### Behaviors

* Learns in short sessions around other responsibilities.
* Works on practical projects.
* Revisits topics multiple times.
* Uses multiple learning resources.
* Values historical records of progress.

### Frustrations

* Short-term productivity tools do not represent long-term learning well.
* Previous learning experiences are easily forgotten.
* It is difficult to see how individual study sessions contribute to larger goals.
* Learning progress is often measured only by course completion.

### Needs from Mezgeb

* Long-lived goals.
* Progress history.
* Learning session records.
* Reflections and journal entries.
* Historical activity.
* A clear relationship between daily learning and long-term goals.

# 5. Common User Characteristics

Although Mezgeb may serve different types of learners, its primary users share several characteristics.

### They are self-directed

Users often decide what they want to learn and how they want to learn it.

### They have long-term goals

Learning goals may take weeks or months rather than a single session.

### They use multiple resources

Users may combine:

* Courses
* Books
* Documentation
* Videos
* Articles
* Tutorials
* Projects
* Practice problems
* University materials

### They care about progress

Users want to understand whether their learning is actually moving forward.

### They benefit from reflection

Simply completing a task does not capture everything learned. Users may want to record:

* What they learned
* What was difficult
* What they still do not understand
* What they want to revisit
* What they should do next

# 7. Persona Prioritization

### Primary

**Independent Technical Learner**

The product should primarily optimize for users who independently learn technical skills over extended periods.

### Secondary

**Structured Student**

This persona represents university students who want to combine academic learning with independent learning.

### Secondary

**Long-Term Skill Builder**

This persona represents users who have moved beyond purely academic learning and are continuously developing professional skills.

# 8. Design Implications

These personas lead to several important product decisions.

### 8.1 Learning should be more important than task completion

A completed task is useful, but it should not be the only representation of progress.

Mezgeb should connect tasks with sessions, reflections, and goals.

### 8.2 Long-term progress should remain visible

Users may work toward a goal for months.

The interface should therefore make it easy to understand both:

* What needs to be done now
* How far the user has progressed overall

### 8.3 Reflection should be lightweight

Users should be able to record reflections without feeling like they are writing an essay after every study session.

### 8.4 The product should avoid unnecessary productivity pressure

Mezgeb can show activity and consistency without making the experience feel like a competition.

### 8.5 The interface should support multiple learning goals

Users are likely to learn several subjects simultaneously, so the application should make switching between goals simple.

---

# 9. Persona Principle

> **Mezgeb should help learners understand their learning journey, not simply count the things they completed.**
